# Contributing

This repository accepts bounded research input about Repàs Actiu educational-content quality.

## Preferred submission

Open a GitHub Issue with:

```text
Claim under test:
Minimal counterexample:
Expected behavior:
Observed/predicted failure:
Deterministic assertion or falsifier:
Evidence/provenance:
Known limitations:
```

Longer research material may be proposed as Markdown under `experiments/` or another clearly research-only path.

## Rules

- Keep submissions self-contained and falsifiable.
- Prefer synthetic/minimal fixtures over source-material reproduction.
- Do not include course PDFs, answer keys, private mappings, personal data, credentials, or secrets.
- Do not add executable workflows, deployment configuration, or production automation.
- External claims are not academic authority.
- There is no automatic production promotion path.

By contributing repository content, you agree that your contribution may be distributed under the repository's Apache-2.0 license, to the extent you have rights to grant that license.
